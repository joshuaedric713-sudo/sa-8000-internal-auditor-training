What Is SA 8000 Internal Auditor Training?

SA 8000 Internal Auditor Training is designed to help professionals understand social accountability principles and assess whether workplace practices meet the applicable requirements of the SA 8000 standard.

The training introduces participants to important areas such as child labour, forced or compulsory labour, health and safety, freedom of association, discrimination, disciplinary practices, working hours, and remuneration.

Participants learn how to prepare audit plans, conduct interviews, review records, identify nonconformities, and communicate audit findings. These skills support organizations in monitoring and improving their social accountability management systems.

Key Learning Areas

  • Understanding SA 8000 requirements
  • Social performance evaluation
  • Risk-based audit preparation
  • Interviewing and evidence collection
  • Audit findings and corrective actions
  • Internal audit reporting

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about SA 8000 Internal Auditor Training.

What is SA 8000 Internal Auditor Training?

SA 8000 Internal Auditor Training develops the knowledge and skills required to assess an organization's social accountability practices through planned internal audits.

What topics are covered in SA 8000 training?

Topics generally include social accountability principles, labour practices, workplace health and safety, management system requirements, audit planning, evidence collection, nonconformity reporting, and corrective actions.

Who can attend SA 8000 Internal Auditor Training?

Quality professionals, HR personnel, compliance officers, internal auditors, consultants, and other professionals involved in workplace responsibility and management systems can benefit from this training.

Why are internal audits important for SA 8000?

Internal audits help organizations evaluate whether their social accountability processes are implemented effectively, identify gaps, and monitor corrective actions.

Does completing the training automatically make an organization SA 8000 certified?

No. Internal auditor training develops individual auditing knowledge. It does not automatically certify an individual or an organization. Organizational certification involves a separate assessment process.

What is the difference between SA 8000 awareness and internal auditor training?

Awareness training introduces the standard and its requirements. Internal auditor training goes further by developing practical skills for planning, conducting, documenting, and reporting internal audits.
